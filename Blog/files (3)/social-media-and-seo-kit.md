**FAQ section for the blog** (add near the end of the post — targets Google's "People Also Ask" and powers the FAQ schema below)
- **Is it better to rent or buy a house in India?** In major metros, EMIs run 2–3.5× the rent for the same home and gross rental yields sit under 3.5%. A renter who invests the difference at 12–14% CAGR can finish 1.85–2.61× wealthier over 20 years.
- **What is the real upfront cost of a ₹1.2 crore home?** Around ₹52.2 lakh in liquid capital — a ₹24 lakh down payment, ₹7.2 lakh stamp duty and registration, ₹6 lakh builder charges, and ₹15 lakh in interiors.
- **What happens to my home loan EMI when interest rates rise?** Indian banks typically keep the EMI fixed and silently extend your tenure instead — a 1.5% rate rise can add 6–8 years to a 20-year loan.
- **How long do Indian apartments actually last?** The functional lifespan of modern Indian apartments is typically 40–60 years due to concrete carbonation, corrosion, and water infiltration.
