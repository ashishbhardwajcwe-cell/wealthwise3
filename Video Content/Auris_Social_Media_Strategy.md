## 3. The storytelling formula you'll use for every video

You asked for: hook → plot → climax. Here's the production version, calibrated for 12-40 minute videos:

### The 6-beat formula (works for every video)

**Beat 1 — The Hook (0:00-0:45)**
- Open in the middle of a story, not at the beginning
- Example: *"In 2009, a US Marine retired with a $400,000 lump sum. Eleven years later, he was living on his daughter's couch. This is what he got wrong — and the three rules every veteran should follow before they touch that money."*
- Promise the payoff but DO NOT reveal the answer

**Beat 2 — The Stakes (0:45-2:00)**
- Why this matters to the viewer specifically
- "If you're within 5 years of retirement, transition, or a windfall, this will save you years of regret"
- This is where you set up curiosity that pulls them through the long video

**Beat 3 — The Plot / Backstory (2:00-8:00)**
- Walk through the situation in detail
- Build emotional investment — names (changed), specific numbers, specific decisions
- Layer in 2-3 mini-cliffhangers ("but here's what he didn't know yet...")

**Beat 4 — The Climax / Turning Point (8:00-15:00)**
- The decision that defined the outcome
- The lesson surfaces here, but is not yet fully explained
- This is where you reveal the *what* but withhold the deeper *why*

**Beat 5 — The Framework / Lesson (15:00-25:00)**
- The 3-5 transferable principles from the story
- This is where the educational value lives
- Use the WealthWise app as a natural example — *"if you'd plugged these numbers into WealthWise's projection tool, it would have shown..."*

**Beat 6 — The Open Loop (25:00-end)**
- Leave one question unanswered
- Tease the next video: *"Next week, I'm telling the opposite story — a Lieutenant Colonel who turned his pension into a $4M portfolio. The two decisions that made the difference."*
- CTA: "If you want to model your own situation, the WealthWise planner is free at auriswealth.co"

### Why this works for 2026 YouTube

- **Long video, sustained retention** — YouTube's algorithm rewards watch time. A 25-minute video held to the end beats a 10-minute video that loses people at 4 minutes.
- **Open loops drive subscriptions** — viewers who feel "I need the next one" subscribe at 3-5x the rate
- **Specific stories beat generic advice** — generic finance advice is over-supplied; specific narrative is rare and AI-template channels can't replicate it well

## 6. The American English voiceover — how to do this right

You said voiceover should be American English. Three options, ranked:

**Option 1 (recommended): Clone your own voice in American accent.**
- Descript Overdub or ElevenLabs both let you clone YOUR voice
- Then use ElevenLabs' accent adjustment OR have your script written in a way that an Indian English voice still sounds confident and global
- **My honest recommendation:** Don't fake American accent. Use your real voice. A retired Colonel with an Indian accent speaking confidently in clear English is more trustworthy than a fake American voice. The trust premium beats the accent premium.

**Option 2: Use a preset American English voice from ElevenLabs**
- Voices like "Adam," "Antoni," or "Rachel" are widely used
- Pick ONE and stick to it for every video — audiences bond with voices
- Risk: dozens of finance channels use the same voices. You sound like everyone else.

**Option 3: Hire a US voice actor on Fiverr/Voices.com**
- $50-200 per video for a 25-30 minute script
- Highest trust but slowest production
- Use only for hero videos (1 in 5)

**Strong recommendation: use your real voice for at least 1 video per month — the "founder voice" video. This protects you against YouTube's AI-content flags entirely.**

## 7. How to avoid YouTube taking down your AI-assisted content

This is the most important section. Read carefully.

### The 7 rules that keep you safe

**Rule 1 — One person, one face, one voice.**
The channel must be obviously tied to a real human. Use your face in the channel banner, the "About" page, and at least the intro of every 5th video. Your photo and name in the channel description.

**Rule 2 — Vary the structure every video.**
Do not use the same intro template, same outro template, same b-roll style. Even small variation each video tells YouTube's classifier "this is a human deciding what to make, not a template running on a cron job."

**Rule 3 — Original research, original framing.**
Every video must contain at least one piece of analysis, story, or data that doesn't appear on the first 10 Google results for that topic. Your military experience + WealthWise client work is your source of original material.

**Rule 4 — Disclose AI usage where required.**
In YouTube Studio, under each video, mark the "altered or synthetic content" toggle IF you use AI-generated visuals that could be mistaken for real footage, OR voice clone of a real person who isn't you. This disclosure does NOT demonetise you; failure to disclose can.

**Rule 5 — Human review every script before recording.**
Read every AI-generated script out loud. Cut the generic parts. Add a personal anecdote (one is enough). Re-order something. This is what passes YouTube's "human creative input" test.

**Rule 6 — Cap upload velocity at 5 per month maximum.**
The "mass produced" flag triggers above ~10 uploads per day with template similarity, but for safety stay well below 2 per week. Your 5/month target is perfect — it signals quality.

**Rule 7 — Mix media types.**
Some videos with talking head (your face on camera). Some with AI voiceover + visuals. Some with screen recordings of WealthWise. This variety alone tells YouTube you're a creator, not a content farm.

### The 3 things that get channels demonetised in 2026

1. **Same template, same voice, every video.** 50 videos that look identical.
2. **Pure AI scripts read by pure AI voice over pure stock footage with no commentary layer.**
3. **High upload frequency** combined with the above.

Avoid all three and you're fine. Your real-Colonel-with-real-stories approach is the opposite of what they're hunting.

## 10. The trust-score strategy

You said "high trust score." This is not a YouTube metric — it's a market perception. Here's how you build it:

1. **Use your face and name everywhere.** The "Retired Colonel" framing is your trust anchor. Wear it on the channel banner, the website, every video intro.

3. **Disclaimers in every video.** "I am not a registered investment advisor in your jurisdiction. This is education, not advice. Please consult your local advisor." This actually INCREASES trust — viewers know you're playing it straight.
4. **Reply to every comment for the first 90 days.** Personal engagement signals authenticity.
5. **Bring guests.** A 30-min interview with another retired officer who became a financial advisor is gold — trust transfers from them to you.
6. **Live streams once a month.** Live = real human. Even a 30-min Q&A live builds enormous trust.
7. **Show client outcomes (anonymised, with permission).** "Here's what we built for one of our clients last quarter" — nothing builds trust faster.

## 13. The honest risks and how to manage them

**Risk 1: It takes longer than 6 months to monetise.** Finance niche channels often hit YPP eligibility (1000 subs + 4000 watch hours) in 3-6 months, but the real money kicks in around 25,000-50,000 subs. Budget for 9-12 months of effort before significant revenue.

**Risk 2: SEBI advertising rules.** Even on YouTube, if you advise on Indian securities while promoting Auris, SEBI rules apply. Every video must include the disclaimer. Avoid specific buy/sell language until your RIA licence is live.

**Risk 3: Burnout.** 5 videos/month at 25-30 minutes each is roughly 15-20 hours of work per video using AI tools. That's 75-100 hours/month — a serious side commitment on top of running Auris. Solve this by hiring a junior editor by month 3 (₹25,000-50,000/month freelancer).

**Risk 4: AI policy changes again.** YouTube tightens rules quarterly now. Stay subscribed to TubeBuddy and VidIQ newsletters. The minute they announce a new rule, audit your last 10 videos against it.

**Risk 5: Your face on camera for a global audience.** This is a one-way door — once you're a public figure, you're committed. Make sure your family agrees and Auris's compliance team is comfortable.
